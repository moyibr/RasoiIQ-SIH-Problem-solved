# SIH 2026 Demo Script: RasoiIQ

Yeh script RasoiIQ ke actual codebase (backend + frontend) par aadharit hai. Har technical term aur feature flow strictly wahi hai jo system me implemented hai. Total duration: ~6 minutes.

| Step | Screen/Page | KYA DIKHANA HAI (Action) | KYA BOLNA HAI (Narration) | Duration |
|------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|----------|
| 1 | **Landing / Login Page** (`/` ya `/login`) | Hero section aur Login button par point karein. | "Welcome to RasoiIQ, hamara AI-powered food reduction aur rescue management system. Yahan se Kitchens, NGOs, aur Volunteers securely login karke apne respective dashboards access kar sakte hain." | 20s |
| 2 | **Signup Page** (`/signup`) | Role selection dropdown (Donor, NGO, Volunteer) highlight karein. | "System me strict role-based access control (RBAC) hai. Har stakeholder (jaise Donor ya NGO) ka apna tailored view aur functionality hoti hai, jisse data isolation aur security maintain rehti hai." | 20s |
| 3 | **Donor Dashboard** (`/donor` ya `/processing-unit`) | Active "Rescue" postings aur "Log Food" action par dhyaan dein. | "Koi bhi kitchen apna surplus khana yahan log karta hai, jise humne 'Surplus' se 'Rescue' me rename kiya hai. Ye data turant backend me FastAPI aur SQLite database me store ho jata hai aur NGOs ko broadcast hota hai." | 25s |
| 4 | **Andaza (Forecasting) Page** (`/andaza` ya `/dashboard`) | Forecast chart aur "ANYA Ai ENGINE" badge par click karein. | "Yeh hamara 'Andaza' module hai jo demand forecast karta hai. Iske backend me XGBoost Poisson regression model chal raha hai jo 47 features (jaise weather, seasonality, aur location) ko analyze karta hai. Iska tested Mean Absolute Error (MAE) 48.8 customers per day hai." | 35s |
| 5 | **Quality Check Page** (`/quality`) | Image upload karein aur Quality Score result dikhayein. | "Food quality ensure karne ke liye, hum backend me OpenCV ka use karke CPU-based lightweight image analysis karte hain. Yeh RGB image ko HSV space me convert karta hai aur dark spots ya discoloration identify karke freshness score deta hai." | 30s |
| 6 | **Volunteer Dashboard** (`/volunteer`) | Available Rescue events aur "Accept" action dikhayein. | "Volunteers yahan aas-paas ki available food rescue requests dekh sakte hain aur unhe accept kar sakte hain. Yeh realtime coordination ensure karta hai taaki khana kharab hone se pehle NGOs tak pahunch jaye." | 25s |
| 7 | **IoT Monitor** (`/iot-monitor`) | Temperature/Humidity charts aur warning indicators dikhayein. | "Food safety standards maintain karne ke liye, hamara IoT monitor storage conditions ko track karta hai. Agar temperature threshold cross karta hai, toh system automatically critical alerts generate karta hai." | 25s |
| 8 | **Live Dispatch & Map** (`/map`) | Route optimization line aur "Waypoints" list dikhayein. | "Logistics ke liye hum Google's OR-Tools ka use karke Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (VRPTW) solve karte hain. Yeh OSRM matrices ke through shortest aur sabse efficient delivery route calculate karta hai. Time window strict hone par ye nearest-neighbor algorithm par fallback karta hai." | 35s |
| 9 | **ESG Report** (`/esg-report`) | "CO2 Avoided", "Meals Donated", aur "Food Saved" charts. | "Hamara ESG Report page environmental impact track karta hai. FAO guidelines ke anusaar, hum calculate karte hain ki bachaye gaye khane se kitna carbon footprint (CO2e) kam hua aur kitni meals donate ki gayin." | 25s |
| 10 | **Closing / Tech Stack** (End Screen) | Project architecture ya simple "Thank You" slide dikhayein. | "To summarize, hamara frontend Next.js 14 aur TailwindCSS se bana hai, aur backend Python me FastAPI par chal raha hai. AI/ML ke liye humne XGBoost, OpenCV, OR-Tools, aur Anthropic APIs integrate kiye hain. Thank you!" | 25s |
